A note on brewing

Green: 80°C · 2½ min. Oolong: 90°C · 3 min. Black: 98°C · 3 min. Chamomile: 100°C · 5 min. For a real cup, follow your tea’s packet if it differs. Heating here is an illustration; start steeping when your real water is ready.
